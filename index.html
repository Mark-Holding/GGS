Good to know

Questions we'reoften asked.

Anything else? Send us a message or give us a call — we're happy to chat things through.

How much does landscaping cost?

Every garden is different, so we quote each job individually — and the quote is completely free with no obligation. A patio, new lawn or clearance is priced on size, access and materials, and regular maintenance visits work out more cost-effective than one-off tidies. Either way you get a fixed price before any work starts.

Do I need to be home while you work?

Not at all. As long as we can access the garden, most customers simply leave us to it. We'll send you a message when we've finished.

Do you take the waste away?

Yes — old slabs, rubble, soil, clippings and clearance waste are all removed and disposed of responsibly. You won't be left with a pile to deal with.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance, so you're covered and can have complete peace of mind.

Do you do maintenance as well as landscaping?

Yes. Many customers start with a patio, lawn or clearance and then ask us to keep the garden looking good afterwards. We can set up visits at whatever frequency suits you — with no long contracts.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain rarely stops us, but if the weather makes a job unsafe or would damage your lawn, we'll let you know in advance and rearrange for the next available slot.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, card or cash — whichever is easiest for you. Regular customers can pay monthly.
