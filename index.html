Good to know

Questions we're often asked.

Anything else? Send us a message or give us a call — we're happy to chat things through.

How much does garden maintenance cost?

Every garden is different, so we quote each job individually — and the quote is completely free with no obligation. Regular visits work out more cost-effective than one-off jobs, and we'll always give you a fixed price before any work starts.

Do I need to be home while you work?

Not at all. As long as we can access the garden, most customers simply leave us to it. We'll send you a message when we've finished.

Do you take the garden waste away?

Yes — all clippings, prunings and clearance waste are removed and disposed of responsibly. You won't be left with bags to deal with.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance, so you're covered and can have complete peace of mind.

Can I book a one-off job rather than regular visits?

Of course. Many customers start with a one-off tidy or clearance. If you'd like us to keep it looking good afterwards, we can set up visits at whatever frequency suits you — with no long contracts.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain rarely stops us, but if the weather makes a job unsafe or would damage your lawn, we'll let you know in advance and rearrange for the next available slot.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, card or cash — whichever is easiest for you. Regular customers can pay monthly.
